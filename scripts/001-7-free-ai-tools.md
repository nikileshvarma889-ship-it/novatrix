**Before publishing — accuracy checks**

- "Free" means a free tier or free plan with limits (daily credits, message caps, watermarks). Consider adding a quick on-screen note: *"Free plans available — limits apply."*
- Usage rights on free plans vary. ElevenLabs and Suno free tiers have historically restricted commercial use or required attribution; check current terms before encouraging viewers to monetise output.
- Re-check each tool's current pricing page the week you publish — plans change often.

**Optional script tweaks**

- The intro promises number 7 is "seriously impressive", but Canva is the best-known tool on the list. Either open the Canva segment with a stronger hook (e.g. show a full thumbnail made in seconds with Magic Design), or soften the teaser to "stay until the end for a bonus tip."
